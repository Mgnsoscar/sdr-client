Anchor direction cues

Marking each step with a role fails, because one step's edge does both at once: BW 20 below is anchored to GPS (one line in) AND is the anchor for three steps (three lines out) — all meeting at the same pin. A single "anchor / anchorer" badge can't say both.

The role isn't a property of the step — it's a property of each line's ends. So mark direction on the line: the head sits on the dependent (the anchorer), the tail on the anchor. Then a junction reads itself — an arrow pointing into a step means it's anchored; arrows leaving mean steps hang off it — however many. Below, the same 1-in / 3-out junction under each treatment.
