What changed vs today:
  • Per signal: a Measurement (one quantity + unit, its frequency, its curve) and a Limiting reading. No Reported field.
  • The parameter form gets the measurement quantity + unit, plus any derived-law quantities, to control --power by (quantity [unit] above the field).
  • Limiting always resolves to dBm: Same as measurement (only if measured in dBm) · Derived (a law that returns dBm) · Separate measurement (a dBm curve).
  • The ceiling left the signal. One dBm limit on the Source stage output (its limits list) caps every signal's limiting reading.
