Two lines, one window

The Hold is a window, not a mark: enter hold → resume. Its width is set live on Proceed, so it wears the relative hatch and carries no ticks inside — the task holds its state across it.

Snap to an edge

Anchor a step to the Hold and its offset picks the side: < 0 lands on the enter line (before the hold), ≥ 0 on the resume line (after it). Nothing can be dropped inside the window.

Anchors like any step

The Hold itself anchors to on-air, off-air, or another step’s edge (a hold right after a ramp’s end) with an offset — and later, to another Hold. Any step can anchor to it in return.

Drag offset from Hold across zero to watch the new step jump between the two edges. Switch the Hold’s own anchor to see the whole window move while the edge-steps ride along.
